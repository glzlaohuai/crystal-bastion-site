Crystal Bastion Privacy Policy

Updated October 28, 2026

Crystal Bastion is an offline single-player game. The developer does not collect player data, operate game accounts, display ads, or use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Progress, settings, unfinished battles, and a limited number of diagnostic events are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to developer servers. Uninstalling the app removes this local data.

On iOS, you may sign in to Apple Game Center. The game then reports local achievement progress to Apple through GameKit. Apple handles this service under its own terms and privacy policy. The game does not send your Apple player identity to the developer, and Game Center does not restore or alter local saved games. Declining sign-in or playing offline does not block gameplay.

The game does not request access to location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, Bluetooth, or advertising tracking. Network access is only needed for optional Apple Game Center services.

The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other players.

If future versions add accounts, advertising, or other data processing, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before those features are released.
