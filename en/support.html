Crystal Bastion Support

Updated October 28, 2026

Thank you for playing Crystal Bastion. The game supports offline play and stores progress on your device.

To resume a battle, save and return home from the pause menu, then choose Resume. Deleting the app removes local progress.

Choose Language in the home Settings or battle pause menu. System language follows your device; unsupported languages use English.

Music, sound effects, ambience, and reduced effects can be adjusted in Settings.

Optional Game Center achievements do not restore saves or synchronize progress across devices. You can play without signing in.

For help, email 494763134@qq.com with your device model, operating system, game version, level, and steps to reproduce the issue.
